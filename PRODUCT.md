## Product Purpose

Yullab is **one person's personal brand / research log**, not a multi-person company or team lab. Corrected 2026-09-17 (was previously drafted as a team-lab site; the site must not imply a team that doesn't exist). The site documents an individual's path:
- As an **architect (설계자) working toward MCP expertise** — this part is solo, and is framed as recording/sharing ("기록하고 공유합니다"), not "we research together."
- As a **researcher who uses and learns AI Agents together with the broader community** — this part legitimately uses "함께" (together), since it's about learning alongside other researchers/developers, not claiming an internal team.

Success is primarily proof-of-capability (a credible, well-crafted showcase of research and skill) rather than a hard sales funnel — though it should still support the "AI integration expert" positioning for visitors evaluating a partner.

## Positioning

An individual positioned specifically around AI Agents and MCP (Model Context Protocol) — a narrower, more technically current focus than generic "AI consulting," signaling depth in agentic AI integration rather than broad AI services. Not an agency/team; copy must not use "저희" (we, corporate) or "팀" language that implies a company.
